Diário News

Automated short-form news video generator

Diário News is a personal automation tool that selects a current news headline, generates a short narrated script, produces a vertical video, and publishes it to a single authorized TikTok account.

  • Content source: public news RSS feeds
  • Script generation: AI-assisted, in the operator's own words
  • Narration: automated text-to-speech
  • Publishing: TikTok Content Posting API (single authorized account)
