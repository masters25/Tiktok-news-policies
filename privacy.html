Privacy Policy

Last updated: [August 12 2026]

This Privacy Policy explains how [Diario news] ("the App", "we") handles information when you use it. The App is a personal automation tool that generates short news-summary videos and publishes them to a single authorized TikTok account.

1. Information we access

When you connect your TikTok account to the App via TikTok's official Login Kit / OAuth 2.0 flow, we request only the minimum permissions required to publish video content on your behalf (the video.publish scope). We do not request access to your TikTok direct messages, contacts, or any data unrelated to publishing content.

2. How we use information

The access token obtained through TikTok's OAuth flow is used exclusively to upload and publish videos that the App generates automatically, to the same account that authorized the connection. We do not use this access for any other purpose, and we do not access or process content from any other TikTok account.

3. Data storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository's secret store and are not accessible to anyone other than the App owner. We do not maintain a separate database of user data.

4. Third-party services

To generate video content, the App sends publicly available news headlines to Google's Gemini API (for script generation) and to Microsoft's text-to-speech service (for voice narration). These services process only the generated news script text — never your TikTok account credentials or personal data.
